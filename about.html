About Bliss Out Dance Studio

Bliss Out Dance Studio is an offline dance studio located in Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh, dedicated to teaching traditional and expressive Garba dance forms.

Founded with the belief that dance is more than movement, Bliss Out focuses on rhythm, confidence, coordination, and cultural connection. Our studio welcomes dancers of all age groups, including kids, teens, and adults.

Currently, Bliss Out Dance Studio specializes in Garba classes in Khandwa, offering structured evening batches for beginners, intermediate learners, and advanced dancers.

Why Choose Bliss Out?

  • ✔ Dedicated Garba-focused training
  • ✔ Structured batch timings
  • ✔ Friendly and inclusive environment
  • ✔ Offline studio experience in Khandwa

Our Vision

Bliss Out Dance Studio aims to build a strong and inclusive dance community in Khandwa, where rhythm, culture, and self-expression come together. Our vision is to make Garba accessible to everyone — from beginners discovering dance for the first time to advanced dancers refining their craft.

We believe dance is not just performance, but a way to grow confidence, discipline, and togetherness. Through consistent practice, supportive teaching, and cultural connection, Bliss Out strives to become a space where dancers feel inspired, empowered, and at home.
