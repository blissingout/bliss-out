About Bliss Out

Bliss Out is an offline dance studio based in Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh. We focus on Garba as a form of rhythm, expression, and cultural connection.

Our space welcomes beginners discovering dance for the first time, as well as experienced dancers refining their movement and confidence.

At Bliss Out, dance is not about perfection — it is about presence, discipline, and joy.
